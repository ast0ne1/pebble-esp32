Pebble

Flash firmware to the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75C over USB.

Use Chrome or Edge on desktop — Web Serial is required. Open this page over http://localhost or HTTPS.
  1. Hold BOOT, plug USB, then release (download mode).
  2. Click Connect & flash and pick the ESP32-S3 COM port.
  3. First install: erase when prompted (clears NVS / Wi‑Fi).
  4. Rebuild merge bin after firmware changes: python tools/mkflash.py

Board must be the 1.75C (32 MB). Plain 1.75 is not supported.
